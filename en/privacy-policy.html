Privacy policy

Data controller

Domaine de la Poterie, 1370 route de Pont-l’Évêque, 14800 Tourgéville, France. Contact: fabricearosio@gmail.com · +33 6 99 21 89 41.

Data collected

Through the contact and booking request forms: first name, last name, email address, phone number (optional), preferred dates, number of guests and message.

Purposes and legal basis

This data is used solely to respond to your request and, where applicable, to prepare your stay. Processing is based on your consent and on pre-contractual measures taken at your request.

Recipients

The data is intended for Domaine de la Poterie. It passes through the form-sending service: Smoobu GmbH. It is never sold or transferred.

Retention period

Correspondence is kept for as long as necessary to process the request and, at most, three years after the last contact, subject to accounting and legal obligations relating to a stay.

Your rights

In accordance with the GDPR and the French Data Protection Act, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details above. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr), the French data protection authority.

Cookies and third-party content

This site does not set any advertising or audience-measurement cookies. Some pages display third-party content that may collect technical connection data (IP address): the Google Maps map (Practical info page) and Google Fonts typefaces.

Video surveillance

No video surveillance cameras are installed on the estate.
