The art of living at home

A family home, run by a family

Domaine de la Poterie was born of a love of tradition, hospitality and good company. We run it ourselves, and you can feel it in the welcome as much as in the attention to detail.

In the heart of the Pays d’Auge, 5 km from Deauville, the house is yours exclusively: family holidays, weekends with friends, birthdays or small corporate retreats.

Pool · terrace · tennis

The joy of being outdoors

In fine weather, life carries on around the heated pool, on the large travertine terrace and on the private tennis court.

Bedrooms

Seven bedrooms, each with its own bathroom

Renovated in 2022, spread over three floors, each with a private bathroom. Room for fifteen guests, each at their own pace.

Autumn · winter

By the fireside

As the days draw in, the house gathers around the large Norman stone fireplace. Long dinners in the dining room, afternoons of reading, the suite’s sauna after a walk in the countryside.

Just minutes away, Deauville shows another face: the beach all to yourself at low tide, the scent of oysters at the market, hot chocolate facing the sea.

Surroundings

Between Deauville and the Pays d’Auge

On one side, the Côte Fleurie with its boardwalk and harbours. On the other, stud farms, orchards and half-timbered villages. The house sits between the two.
