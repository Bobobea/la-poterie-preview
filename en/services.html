Services

Everything is ready for you to enjoy the house

Renovated in 2022, the house is equipped so that everyone feels at home from the moment they arrive.

01

Included in your stay

Nothing to bring, nothing to arrange before you leave.

  • Bed linen
  • Bath towels
  • Pool towels
  • Terre de Mars toiletries in the bathrooms
  • End-of-stay cleaning
  • Welcome on site by Jennifer, your contact throughout your stay
02

Enjoying the estate

The park, the water and games, without leaving the property.

  • Pool heated in season, with electric cover, sun loungers and parasol
  • Private hard tennis court
  • Sauna in the suite
  • Table-tennis table, bats and balls
  • 3-hectare wooded park, maintained all year round
03

At the table

Everything you need to cook for and host the whole party, indoors and out.

  • Equipped kitchen: two dishwashers, American-style fridge, oven, microwave, gas cooker, Nespresso machine
  • Dining room for the whole household
  • Travertine terrace of around 200 m²
  • Large outdoor table with parasol for 14 to 16 guests
  • Charcoal barbecue
04

To make your stay easier

The little things that make all the difference, especially with family.

  • Travel cot and high chair
  • Books, board games and children’s toys
  • Laundry room with two washing machines and tumble dryer
  • Wi-Fi and workspace
  • Free parking within the enclosed property
  • Mid-stay cleaning on request, for longer stays
  • A special request? Contact us in advance

A house for coming together

Family reunion, intimate birthday, stay with friends or business trip: for any special arrangements, contact us in advance. Out of respect for the neighbours, the house does not host noisy parties.
